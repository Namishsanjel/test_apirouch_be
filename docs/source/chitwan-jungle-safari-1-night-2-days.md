## Trip overview

Chitwan National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site in Nepal’s southern lowlands and one of the best places in Asia to see wildlife. The park shelters the one-horned rhinoceros, the Bengal tiger, Asian elephants, sloth bears, gharial and marsh mugger crocodiles and hundreds of bird species. This one-night, two-day program is a compact way to enjoy it, with a jeep safari, a canoe ride, a jungle walk and a cultural evening in the village life of the Tharu people.

It is a good add-on to a Kathmandu or Pokhara trip, and it can be a break between city sightseeing and trekking. You are accompanied by a naturalist guide, and all meals, park fees and activities are covered.

- Driver collects you at your hotel in Kathmandu or Pokhara at about 7:00 a.m.
- Drive to Chitwan through river valleys and the green lowlands of the Terai
- Journey takes roughly four to six hours
- Depends on your starting city and traffic
- Check in at your lodge and enjoy lunch
- Afternoon jeep safari into the park with your naturalist
- A good time for wildlife, as animals become active in the cooler hours
- Look for rhinos, spotted deer, wild boar, monkeys and colorful birds
- After dark, watch the Tharu cultural program
- Stick dances and drum music
- Dine at the lodge
- Overnight stay in the lodge

### Day 2: Canoe ride, jungle walk, elephant breeding center and return

- After breakfast at about 7:30 a.m. board a dugout canoe
- Quiet ride along the river
- Spot crocodiles, kingfishers and other birds on the banks
- Guided walk through the forest with your naturalist
- Learn about animal tracks, plants and safety rules
- Visit the elephant breeding center
- Learn about the park's conservation work
- Back at the lodge, freshen up and check out
- Drive back to Kathmandu or Pokhara
- Dropped at your hotel

## What to pack

## Best time to visit

October to April is the dry season, when vegetation is thinner and animals gather near water. Spring shows flowers and active wildlife, and winter has cool mornings and clear air. The monsoon, from June to August, makes tracks muddy and sightings harder.

## Frequently asked questions

**What wildlife can I expect to see?** One-horned rhinos are the most reliable sighting, along with deer, monkeys, crocodiles and many birds. Tigers and sloth bears live in the park but are rarely seen.

**Is Chitwan safe?** Yes, when you follow your guide’s instructions, stay on designated routes, keep a respectful distance from animals and keep noise low.

**What are the best hours for wildlife?** Early morning and late afternoon are the most active times.

**Can I extend my stay?** Yes. Longer programs with more safaris, birdwatching and a tower night stay are available, and you can continue to Lumbini, Pokhara or Bardia.
