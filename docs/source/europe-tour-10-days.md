## Day-by-day itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Switzerland

- Arrive at Zurich Airport and meet the representative
- Transfer to the hotel and check in
- Enjoy a relaxed evening and overnight stay in Zurich

### Day 2: Interlaken, Lauterbrunnen and Grindelwald

- Explore Interlaken and its beautiful mountain setting
- Visit Lauterbrunnen Valley and enjoy waterfall views
- Discover Grindelwald and the scenic Swiss Alps
- Overnight stay in the Interlaken area

### Day 3: Walensee, Flumserberg and Appenzell

- Enjoy scenic views of Lake Walensee
- Ride the Flumserberg cable car, subject to operation
- Explore Appenzell, a charming traditional Swiss town
- Overnight stay in Switzerland

### Day 4: Rhine Falls, Zurich and transfer to Milan

- Visit Rhine Falls, one of Europe's most impressive waterfalls
- Enjoy a Zurich city tour or selected highlights
- Transfer to Milan and check in at the hotel
- Overnight stay in Milan

### Day 5: Explore Milan

- Visit Milan's iconic Duomo Cathedral
- Explore Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II
- See Sforza Castle and enjoy free time for shopping
- Overnight stay in Milan

### Day 6: Milan to Venice

- Travel from Milan to Venice
- Enjoy a Grand Canal ride
- Visit St. Mark's Square and see Rialto Bridge
- Overnight stay in Venice

### Day 7: Venice Islands Day Trip

- Visit Murano, famous for its glassmaking tradition
- Explore the colourful houses and canals of Burano
- Visit peaceful Torcello, depending on boat schedules
- Return to Venice for overnight stay

### Day 8: Venice to Paris

- Transfer to the airport and travel to Paris
- Hotel transfer and check-in
- Enjoy an evening stroll along the Seine, time permitting
- Overnight stay in Paris

### Day 9: Paris, Icons and Charm

- Visit the Eiffel Tower area
- Explore Montmartre and its artistic streets
- Enjoy a Seine River cruise, subject to availability
- Overnight stay in Paris

### Day 10: Departure from Paris

- Breakfast and hotel check-out
- Transfer to the airport according to flight timings
- Depart with wonderful memories of your European journey
